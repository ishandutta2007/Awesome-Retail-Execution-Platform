# Awesome-Retail-Execution-Platform

## Top Retail Execution Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Field Force Automation, Merchandising Compliance, Shelf Audits, Image Recognition, Store Visits & CPG Retail Execution*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Retail Execution**. These systems help CPG brands, retailers, and field teams execute in-store strategies—managing store visits, planogram compliance, promotions, shelf audits, order taking, and real-time reporting from the field.



**Examples** include Repsly, StayinFront, Pepperi, GoSpotCheck (FORM), VisitBasis, Trax Retail, ParallelDots, Field Agent, BeMyEye, YOOBIC, Teamcore, BeatRoute, and FORM MarketX (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured retail execution platforms with mobile field apps, AI image recognition for shelves, planogram compliance, and CPG-specific workflows are almost exclusively commercial. Open-source activity is limited to general field-service / forms tools, retail POS/ERP systems that can be extended, and computer-vision experiments. This section lists every significant relevant project and building block found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Repsly](https://www.repsly.com/)**  

  Retail execution and field team management platform popular with CPG brands for merchandising, shelf visibility, store visits, and image-based insights.



- **[Trax Retail](https://traxretail.com/)**  

  Leading AI-powered retail execution and shelf intelligence platform using computer vision for planogram compliance, share-of-shelf, and out-of-stock detection.



- **[GoSpotCheck by FORM / FORM MarketX](https://www.form.com/)**  

  Field execution and audit platform with strong mobile checklists, photo reporting, task management, and image recognition capabilities.



- **[Pepperi](https://www.pepperi.com/)**  

  Combined B2B commerce and retail execution platform supporting field sales, ordering, and in-store execution workflows.



- **[StayinFront, VisitBasis, YOOBIC](https://www.stayinfront.com/)**  

  Established and modern retail execution / field force solutions covering merchandising, audits, and mobile workforce management.



- **[ParallelDots, Field Agent, BeMyEye, Teamcore, BeatRoute](https://www.paralleldots.com/)**  

  Platforms and networks offering image recognition, crowdsourced or field-based retail audits, and execution visibility.



- **[Other retail execution & field force platforms](https://www.repsly.com/)**  

  Additional commercial tools for route-to-market, DSD, and in-store compliance programs.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[SaleFlex](https://saleflex.dev/)**  

  Open-source retail ecosystem (POS, back-office, API hub, mobile) that can serve as a foundation for store-level execution and inventory-related field activities.



- **[OpenRetail](https://github.com/rudi-krsoftware/open-retail)**  

  Open-source retail and POS software oriented toward small-to-medium retail operations; can be extended for basic field or store execution needs.



- **[metasfresh](https://github.com/metasfresh/metasfresh)**  

  Full open-source ERP with inventory, sales, and trade capabilities that some organizations adapt for field order-taking and basic execution processes.



- **[Vendure & headless commerce platforms](https://github.com/vendure-ecommerce/vendure)**  

  Extensible open-source commerce backends that can support custom field-sales or merchandising modules.



- **[Mobile forms & field-service frameworks](https://github.com/search?q=field+service+OR+mobile+forms+OR+inspection+app+open+source)**  

  Open-source mobile form, inspection, and field-service tools that teams adapt for store audits and checklist-based retail execution.



- **[Computer vision & shelf analysis experiments](https://github.com/search?q=shelf+recognition+OR+planogram+OR+retail+shelf+AI)**  

  Research and prototype projects applying image recognition to shelf photos, product detection, and planogram compliance.



- **[Route & task management open tools](https://github.com/search?q=field+force+OR+route+management+OR+sales+force+automation+open+source)**  

  Community projects for basic route planning, visit tracking, and task assignment that can support lightweight field operations.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo field & inventory modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open-source ERPs whose inventory, CRM, and project/task modules are sometimes configured for simple retail execution or merchandising workflows.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Offline-first mobile frameworks**: Tools that support robust offline data capture for store visits in low-connectivity environments.

- **Photo annotation & QA**: Open libraries for capturing, tagging, and reviewing field photos.

- **Checklist & survey engines**: Configurable form builders used for audit and compliance questionnaires.

- **Basic image classification models**: Pre-trained or fine-tunable models for product or shelf-condition detection.

- **Dashboards**: Grafana / Metabase / Superset configured for field KPIs (visit compliance, out-of-stocks, promotion execution).

- Custom stacks combining mobile forms + open ERP + simple CV models for prototype retail execution programs.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

There is no mature, full-featured open-source equivalent to commercial retail execution platforms (Repsly, Trax, GoSpotCheck/FORM, Pepperi, etc.).  

Practical building blocks include mobile forms/field-service frameworks, open ERP/inventory systems (**metasfresh**, **ERPNext**, **Odoo**, **SaleFlex**), and experimental computer-vision projects for shelf analysis.  

Commercial platforms provide purpose-built mobile apps, AI image recognition at scale, CPG-specific workflows, planogram libraries, and enterprise reporting that remain difficult to replicate with open-source components alone.  

Most brands and retailers adopt a commercial retail execution platform and may supplement it with open-source analytics, custom integrations, or internal tools.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Retail execution involves field data collection, photos of store environments, and sometimes competitive intelligence. Privacy, data protection, and accurate representation of in-store conditions are important.

- Open-source tools can support prototypes, internal audits, or lightweight field processes but typically lack the scale, AI accuracy, mobile polish, and industry-specific features of dedicated commercial retail execution platforms. Evaluate operational requirements and total cost of ownership carefully.
